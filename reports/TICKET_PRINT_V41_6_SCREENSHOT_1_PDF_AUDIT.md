# Schedule > Bookings > Passenger Ticket Print and Update v41

Reference: six uploaded screenshots (five ticket-page screenshots and one invoice notice) plus the uploaded three-page A4 portrait Chrome/Skia printed ticket PDF.

## Screenshots and implementation

- Screen 1: operator navbar with gradient initial, operator name, Search, WhatsApp and theme SVG; Ticket/Update segmented control; Get your ticket with Download, Print, Share.
- Screen 1: orange branded ticket banner, operator registered identity, contact, email, office address and GSTIN from saved GDS operator settings, never hard-coded from the reference operator.
- Screen 2: booking ID, PNR, status, service, coach, booking timestamp, boarding/dropping cities, points, instructions, reporting time, contact, map links derived from configured stage coordinates.
- Screen 3: passenger details: passenger, mobile, gender, age, seat, fare; gross fare, discount, net, GST, paid amount, booking source; cancellation policy.
- Screen 4: terms and company footer with operator attribution, Get in Touch, payment labels and support metadata.
- Screen 5: Update tab with Change Pick-up & Drop and Change Mobile cards and working authorized editing forms, via existing permissioned booking PUT endpoint and refreshed ticket.
- Screen 6: upper-right blue-bordered, progress-timed, dismissible Print invoice feature coming soon notice. No invoice is issued.
- PDF: A4 portrait vector ticket with orange header, passenger and fare grid, boarding details, terms, footer and payment labels; three pages for standard small bookings, more pages for larger passenger groups.
- Download saves binary PDF, Print invokes browser PDF printing, Share uses native file-share sheet or fallback download; all operate on the same protected endpoint.

## Security and truthful limits

- Booking data is loaded only through authenticated staff scope and the existing booking permission rules. No public bearer URLs or passenger secrets are copied.
- Customer names, PNRs, third-party manage key URL, contact details or support number from the reference are never hard-coded.
- Operator-specific legal terms and cancellation policy can be configured; defaults are a non-authoritative template requiring operator review.
- Original PDF is a browser print-to-PDF capture with header/footer generated by Chrome; server-generated v41 PDF does not reproduce those browser-only headers.
- Bundled vector renderer uses Helvetica with Rs. currency labels rather than native rupee glyph, so byte-identical or pixel-identical certification is not claimed.
- UI comparison against Chrome at matching viewport and real print/share devices, multi-passenger document verification, and self-service OTP/link design remain distinct production UAT activities.
- No external WhatsApp delivery is claimed by native share or handoff.
- Decorative payment marks do not mean payment gateway integration.
- Importing the reference PDF is not required for GDS; ticket generation uses live authorized booking records.
